# Unsubbed Cycle

**A free alternative to Flo and Clue.** The private period tracker you don't pay for. Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no subscription, no tracking. Your data never leaves your device.

**Use it now:** https://unsubbed.github.io/cycle

## What it does
- Tap a day on the calendar to log your period: how heavy, body symptoms, mood and a note
- See your cycle day, about how many days until your next period, and an estimated fertile window
- The calendar marks logged days, expected periods and likely fertile days with shapes and patterns, not just color
- History table with every cycle's length, your averages and your shortest and longest cycle
- Export a CSV for a spreadsheet, or print a one-page summary to take to a doctor
- Save an encrypted backup locked with your own passphrase
- Open and save your data as an ordinary file on your own device

## Not medical advice
Every number is an estimate based only on the days you log. Unsubbed Cycle is not medical advice and is **not birth control**. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.

## Privacy
Everything happens on your device. The app makes no outside network requests: no analytics, no ads, no server, no account. Your data is stored in this browser until you clear it, and you can save a copy (plain or encrypted) at any time.

## Known limits
No reminders or notifications, no pregnancy mode, no pill tracking, no temperature charts, no sync between devices. Move your data between devices with Save and Open.

## Run it yourself
It's a plain web page. Download this repository and open `index.html`, or serve the folder with any static web server. The encrypted backup needs a secure page (https or localhost).
